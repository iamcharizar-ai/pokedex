## 2. How you actually played

**Pillars by domain over 25 days:** sleep 11, code 6, guitar 6, arbor 4, fitness 2. Momentum never reached x1.5; there was 1 perfect day (13 Oct).

**In-app sessions were bursts, then nothing.** Almost every game action happened on three mornings:

- **13 Oct** (the perfect day): first gym, first catch (Porygon), claims, a Great Ball bought and thrown.
- **19 Oct**: two feats, mastery training, berries.
- **22 Oct**: the big one. Frontier symbol claimed, four domain berries bought and fed to Porygon, Dome Fossil bought, Kabuto and Aerodactyl revived, a Rare Egg bought.

Since 24 Oct the only actions are claiming the daily pay and one Premier Ball. That matches "there's nothing to do": the game has no next action for you, and the one it does have (Meowth) is not being shown as one.

**What gets used:** daily pay, feats, berries (feeding), fossils, mastery training, Frontier passively.
**What has never been touched:** weekly boss, Research desk, Mega (Key Stone sits idle), evolution stones, held gear (Protector / Silver Wing earned from workouts, never equipped), lures/incense wishes, Collector, Palace and Pyramid, the Box-transfer-for-coins option.

## 3. Loopholes and bugs

### 3.1 The "Sleep before 10" pillar does not check bedtime (biggest one)
`core/facts.ts:190` pays the Sleep pillar on the band's sleep score alone (done at 80+). Your bedtimes from the band: 02:40, 02:30, 02:10, 02:20, 01:30, 01:20, 02:00, **04:20**, 06:24. The pillar still counted on 11 days, more than any other domain. On 16 to 20 Oct sleep was the *only* pillar, so it kept the day "active", kept wild and leader damage ticking, and protected you from slips while no real work happened.

It isn't cheating on your part, the rule just measures the wrong thing. Fix options: count it only when `bed` is before a cutoff (or within a window of a target), and pay the score as a bonus on top. Or rename the habit to "Sleep well" and add a separate bedtime pillar. Either way, a 4 AM night should not count as done.

### 3.2 Buttons that don't confirm, so you tap them again and again
- `feat:badge1` claimed **26 times** across 13–14 Oct, a catch quest **9 times**, a perfect feat twice.
- Old Amber "Revive" tapped **10 times** on 22 Oct (06:37 to 07:12) for one Aerodactyl.

The fold ignores the duplicates, so the economy is safe (no exploit). But each tap is a ledger row, and it shows the UI isn't confirming what happened: either it doesn't show the claim as taken until the ledger syncs back, or the Revive tap failed for coins (it needs 150) without saying so. A claim should disappear or tick at once on tap, and a refused action should say why.

### 3.3 Research can never start for a small collection
`deskNow` (`core/fold.ts:519`) takes the Box *minus the party*. The party fills itself from the Box (`TEAM_SIZE` 5), so while you own 5 or fewer the desk is always empty. Research has paid 0 points since v14 shipped, and nothing tells you why. Either let the desk share with the party, or give it its own slot.

### 3.4 Weekly boss locked behind a party of 6
`BOSS_PARTY = 6` (`core/boss.ts:20`) at a real catch rate of about 1 wild Pokemon every 10 days means the boss, a whole weekly system, is invisible for the first month or two. Let it run with whatever party you have and scale it down, or show it greyed out with "needs 2 more Pokemon" so it is a goal and not nothing.

### 3.5 A catch you can make right now, which the app doesn't point at
Meowth has 247 HP left. You hold 4 Great Balls (50 each) and 2 Luxury Balls. Throwing the bag finishes or nearly finishes it today. It has been sitting there 13 days. That is the clearest sign the home screen isn't showing "the one useful thing you can do".

### 3.6 Smaller
- Gear earned from workouts (Protector, Silver Wing) is never equipped: check whether equipping is discoverable or whether the payoff is too small to bother.
- A Key Stone since 19 Oct and no Mega path visible in practice.
- Two dead days in a row (25–26 Oct) put the partner to sleep (`SLEEP_AFTER = 2`). Fine as a rule, but confirm the home screen says it.

## 4. Why it feels like there's nothing to do

The game is a long middle with no small goals inside it:

1. **Levels are the only thing moving, and they pay nothing.** Lv 26 to 36 is ten days of "Level" moments with no reward or choice.
2. **Catching is too slow to feed the other systems.** Boss, Research, party power and Dex all run on owning Pokemon, and you own 5. Everything downstream waits on the one system with the slowest clock.
3. **Currencies pile up with nothing to want.** 38 gems, 19 BP, 19 marks unspent. If a shop has nothing you want, the currency is a number, not a choice (the same test as `game-design-real-choices`).
4. **The real next action is hidden.** Meowth, the egg, the leader weak to fitness: all exist, none is on the front page as "do this today".

## 5. Recommendations

### Do first (small, high value)
1. **Fix the sleep pillar** (3.1). It is the one place the game rewards the opposite of the habit.
2. **A "Today" card at the top of home** with at most three items, picked by the fold: e.g. "Throw at Meowth: 247 HP, you have 4 Great Balls", "Lt. Surge is weak to fitness: a gym session hits x2", "Egg: 14,386 steps to go". This also answers your point about the egg: move the egg's step bar to home (now, not waiting for the overhaul). It is the only thing that moves every day without a tick.
3. **Claim and Revive feedback** (3.2): disable on tap, show the result at once, explain refusals.
4. **Unlock Research and the boss for small collections** (3.3, 3.4), or show them as locked goals with the exact requirement.

### Next (systems that give the middle a shape)
5. **Make catching faster early, slower later.** A wild every ~10 pillar days starves everything. Option: the first five catches take half the HP, or a pillar day also adds a small hit to the wild through the partner's type match.
6. **Weekly focus contract.** Each Monday, pick one domain (or the game picks your weakest, fitness right now). Hitting it 3 times that week pays a chosen reward. This aims the game at what you skip, instead of rewarding what you already do.
7. **Rewards on level milestones.** Every 5 levels: a choice of two items. Turns the ten-day stretch to evolution into five small decisions.
8. **Something to want for gems, BP and marks.** Look at the shelves against what you hold: if nothing on them is worth 38 gems, add a few sinks that are real trade-offs (e.g. reroll today's wild, a held item for the domain you are weakest in, cosmetic themes).
9. **Fitness is your weakest domain (2 workouts in 25 days) and the current leader is weak to it.** That's a nice natural pull. Make it visible: "a workout today does X damage to Lt. Surge vs Y without".

### Later (new ways to progress)
10. **Comeback rule instead of only punishment.** After a slip or sleep, the first full day back pays extra. Your pattern is bursts with gaps, so the game should make returning feel good.
11. **Monthly review as a moment.** You have a Comet Shard from the review; let the month's review show a recap screen (pillars by domain, catches, best streak) and make one choice for next month.
12. **Dex goals by region/type** ("catch 3 Water types"), which gives the lures and incense (never used) a reason to exist.

### For the UI overhaul
- Home = partner + Today card + egg + leader HP. The Bag holds items only.
- Everything you can act on gets a badge dot (claimable feat, throwable wild, ready egg); everything you can't yet gets its requirement in words.
- Hide systems you can't use yet behind one "Coming up" list instead of empty screens.
