# UI/UX audit, 27 Oct 2026

Every tab was captured on a phone (390 px) and a desktop (1280 px) with the `late` and `week` fixtures. The rebuild of 14 Oct (`UI-REVIEW.md`) still holds as a design: sections on the page, one hero per tab, rings and sockets. This audit is about what has gone wrong since. Some of it is plain defects. Most of it is the same fact shown in several places, and pages that grew longer as systems were added.

## The test used

1. **Say a thing once per page.** If a number is on screen already, a second copy is noise. You pointed this out with the Today card, and the same problem is all over the app.
2. **What you can act on comes first.** Reading-only material (perk lists, nightly charts, long shelves) folds or moves down.
3. **A phone is the main screen.** Nothing may clip, overlap or need a sideways scroll to read a label.

## Findings

### Defects
| # | Where | What | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Top bar, phone | With five-digit coins the gem icon sits on top of the coin number ("16,510💎2,367"). Each pill's icon is pushed out to the left over the previous pill. | The icon sits inside the pill. Numbers from 10,000 up are shortened (16.5k) on narrow screens, with the full number in the tooltip. |
| D2 | Battle box, phone | "Wild Ka…": the ball's name ("Great Ball") takes the space, so the Pokemon's name and its rarity chip are cut off. | On a phone the ball shows as its icon only. The name gets the room. |
| D3 | Today | The wild Pokemon's HP was shown three times: the scene's HP box, "Throw and feed · Kabuto · 462 HP", and the Today card. | The Today card goes. The Throw and feed heading only says something new: whether the balls in the bag can finish it. |
| D4 | Today, desktop | The left column ends after the pillar rings while the right column runs three screens. Half the page is empty. | Last night moves to the left column on desktop (it belongs with the day). On a phone it stays at the bottom. |
| D5 | Egg | Its step bar was in three places (Today card, Shop › Rare things, Party › Items › Nursery). The one you look at, home, was the newest copy. | Home shows it as a third tile next to Momentum and Evolves. The Nursery keeps the large view. The copy in the Shop goes. |
| D6 | Shop › Coins | 24 battle items (X items in three strengths, Dire Hits, Guard Spec, Honey, a berry) are always laid out. The Coins counter is 3,600 px tall on a phone, and the Collector sits at the very bottom. | Long shelves show their first row, with "All 24 battle items" to open the rest. The BP Exchange's two longest shelves fold the same way. |

### Structure
| # | Where | What | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Tab bar | Today is the only tab with a reason to open daily. Party and Shop only matter when something there waits on you, and nothing said so. | A gold dot on a tab, and on the screen inside it, when something waits there: a fossil the Lab can afford to bring back, a treasure the Collector pays double for today, a stone that evolves one of your Pokemon. The reason is the tab's tooltip and its screen-reader description. Collect keeps its own button in the top bar. |
| S2 | Party › bond | Five perk paragraphs, plus the hearts said three times (the partner card, the perks header, a bar under the perks). | A track of five perk icons with the hearts each needs. One line says the next perk; tap any icon to read that one. The hearts are said once, on the partner card. |
| S3 | Pokedex | Opens on 185 silhouettes every time, even after you pick "Caught". | The filter is remembered on the device. |

### Left as proposals
- **Frontier cards** repeat the full rule on each of the seven cards. Shorten to the win condition, with the rest in a sheet.
- **The Bag** (Party › Items) is still where items are used. With the dots pointing to it when it matters, it can stay a drawer you visit rarely.

## Result
Page heights on a phone (the `late` fixture): Party 1,684 → 1,401 px, Shop 3,625 → 2,498 px, Today 1,627 → 1,666 px (it gains the egg tile and loses the Today card). The screenshot baselines under `tests/baseline/` were taken again for these changes. Tests, typecheck and the accessibility check pass.
