## 3. Why these bugs keep coming

Fixing the twelve above one by one will not stop new ones. There are three causes underneath.

1. **Two places decide whether an action is allowed.** The screen decides whether to show a button. The rules decide, separately, whether it counts. Nothing forces them to agree, and when they disagree the rules stay silent (bug 2). Every rules change breaks a few buttons this way.
2. **Twenty-one rule versions are all live at once.** Because the game is recomputed from the ledger, old days must replay under old rules. So every version is a date check inside one 2,062-line function (114 version checks in `core/fold.ts`). Each screen has to know which version is in force for every sentence it shows. That is where bugs 3 to 11 come from.
3. **The tests check the rules, not the screens.** 229 tests pass while the screens offer actions the rules refuse and the rule book describes v16. Nothing tests "every button the screen shows does something".

The fixes are in section 8.

## 4. What is wrong with the design

### 4.1 The headline: one input, far too many outputs

You do about four kinds of thing in a day: pillars, chores, sleep, steps. The game turns them into:

- **8 currencies**: coins, gems, Battle Points, and five kinds of marks.
- **At least 14 meters** that move at once: partner XP, wild HP, leader HP, boss HP, research, momentum, the season ladder, two weekly quests, seven Frontier runs, a counter per pillar, egg steps, trail steps, partner hearts, Trainer hearts.
- **234 items**: 14 balls, 10 berries, 38 held items, 28 battle items, 26 evolution items, 26 legend keys, 22 treasures, 17 TMs.
- **17 separate modifiers** on one number, the hit on the gym leader: weakness, type edge, team, bond, Mega, partner level, held item, mastery, nature, vitamins, signature, X item, critical hit, day tier, Type Plate, Expert Belt, Life Orb.

After all 17, a leader falls about every 8 days whatever you do. That is the test for the whole system: most of these parts do not change what you would do tomorrow. They exist, they cost attention, and they are where the bugs live.

It got this way by addition. Almost every one of the 21 versions added more than it took away. The 5 Nov redesign had a second half (cut the weekly quests, the season ladder, the Frontier; at most 4 held items) that was planned as "V22" and never shipped. You are living in half a redesign.

### 4.2 The three bars (your question)

The design is in a comment at the top of `core/fold.ts`:

- how **much** you did moves the partner's XP,
- how **many** habits you ticked wears down the wild Pokemon,
- what **kind** of work you did hits the gym leader.

That is a good idea, and the screens never say it. Worse, the bars are not clean. The leader is hit by every habit including chores, weighted through 17 modifiers. The wild Pokemon is hit by the share of all habits including pillars. So each bar is "mostly" one thing and there is no sentence that is simply true. The boss is clear to you because its card says exactly what counts.

### 4.3 The gym counter (your example, and it is worse than you described)

Your original design: seven routines in the split, each drops its own piece, seven pieces make a Red Shard. One line.

What exists now:

- The kit still drops pieces. A full set pays gems and 3 Iron. Only **every third** set pays a Red Shard (changed in v11).
- v21 added a "counter": 20 Iron reaches for a Red Shard or a Gym Egg.
- **The counter is not a counter.** It is your Iron wallet, and "ready" just means you can afford the item in the Dojo shop underneath. So you saw it twice: once as a choice, once as a price. It is one thing shown two ways.
- That same Iron also buys mastery, balls, TMs, vitamins, a berry and a stone. Buying any of them moves your "counter" backwards. Before the counter existed you spent 12 Iron on Gym mastery (+6% to one of the 17 modifiers). At today's price that was 60% of a Red Shard.
- **The switch between shard and egg does nothing** except change the icon on the Life OS tile. You have tapped it 22 times since 5 Nov.
- **Iron mostly does not come from the gym.** Of the 16 Iron you have ever earned, 13 came from 10,000-step days and 3 from workouts.
- A Red Shard can come from eight places: the shop, every third kit set, a beaten boss, a level milestone, a Ribbon, the 30-day feat, the 100-day run feat, and (as a Comet) the month review.

Arbor and Code have the same problem without the kit.

### 4.4 Berries (you are right)

Oran is 1 heart for 50 coins, Sitrus 3 for 150, Tamato 5 for 250. Exactly 50 coins a heart at every size, so there is no choice, only a bulk pack. The marks shops repeat it (1 mark a heart).

The deeper problem: a normal two-pillar day gives the partner 4 hearts for free, a Max day 11. A whole day's coins (90) buys under 2. Berries sell, slowly, what the day already hands you.

There is one real choice in them, and it is easy to miss: a berry gives double on a day you did that Pokemon's "kind of work".

### 4.5 The Pokemon's journey (your biggest question)

- **Only the partner ever gains levels.** Exp. Share was the last perk on the ladder, and v21 removed it. Your seven others are Lv 1 after 39 days and will stay Lv 1.
- **Training one takes about 80 days** at your pace (15,000 XP at 190 a day). You catch or hatch about one a week. So roughly nine in ten of what you catch will never be raised, and the game never tells you that or what they are for.
- **Finishing a Pokemon is punished.** The partner's level and bond are two of the biggest strike bonuses (+35% and +15% for Charmeleon now). When it reaches Lv 50 the game hands you the oldest Lv 1 in the Box, and your hit on the leader drops by about a third that day.
- **"Lead again to reach Lv 100"** is 45,000 more XP, about 8 months for one Pokemon. Nobody will do this.
- **The Lab desk** holds three. Each adds 10 to 30% to a points meter, the meter fills a rank roughly every week of pillar days, and a rank pays an incense or 20 gems and makes wild Pokemon 3% weaker. It is a job in name only. After three there is nowhere to put anyone.
- **Switching partner does not lose hearts.** Hearts stay with each Pokemon, and the Trainer total never goes down. But it does lose the level and bond bonuses, heavily, and nothing on screen says so. So your instinct that switching costs something is right, for a different reason.

### 4.6 Trainer perks ("what after that?")

Five perks at 10, 40, 100, 200 and 350 hearts. You are at 108, so about two months to the last one. After that the next reward is a Ribbon, which needs all six party members at 150 hearts each (900 in all). Between 350 and 900 there is nothing. And a Pokemon stops at 150, so the total only grows by adding Pokemon. The ladder runs out and the endgame is a long way off.

Two of the perks are odd. The "bounty" pays ×3 or ×4 for a pillar you skipped: it had to be tuned to sit just below the point where skipping beats showing up, which is a warning sign. "Sharing" only matters once a Pokemon is full.

### 4.7 Legend quests

They are not quests. There are 26 keys, and each card says "Gold Symbol at a facility" (a 30-day run) or "250 BP". You have earned about 83 BP in 26 days, 25 of it from a one-off Symbol, so 250 BP is roughly three months per key. It is a price list with a quest's name. The one real route, a Champion handing you a key, only starts on the second lap of all 122 leaders.

### 4.8 Three streak systems on the same data

Momentum, the feats' runs (7, 30, 100 in a row) and the Battle Frontier (a run per pillar) all count "days in a row of pillar X". The weekly quest counts it a fourth way.

### 4.9 Collecting is a ceremony

Rewards wait in Collect, and pay themselves after 3 days. Of your 75 collects, 66 changed nothing about the outcome. Only the ones with a choice mattered.

### 4.10 What is good and should stay

- **The weekly boss.** A card that says exactly what counts. This is the model for everything else.
- **Eggs and the Nursery.** Steps do one thing, in one place.
- **Pickup.** A small surprise, no decision needed, tied to showing up.
- **Sleep** as a measured score.
- **The evolution line** (16, 36, trained at 50) and branching by what you did.
- **The league as the long road**: badges, regions opening.
- **The "nothing is stored" ledger design.** It is why two apps always agree. It is also why rule versions pile up, which section 8 deals with.

## 5. The proposed game ("v22")

One principle: **each thing has one job, each job has one source, and one sentence explains it.** Numbers marked * are first guesses to be tuned in `tools/sim-life.mjs` before anything ships.

### 5.1 The day, in three sentences

> **Every tick trains your partner. The share you ticked catches the wild Pokemon. Pillars fight the gym leader.**

- **Partner XP**: unchanged.
- **Wild Pokemon**: unchanged formula. The catch pace stays, as you asked.
- **Gym leader**: only pillars hit it. 100 a pillar, 150 if the leader is weak to that pillar, × the day's tier (×1, ×1.25, ×1.5, ×2 on a Max day), × your party (5.3). Three terms instead of 17.
- **Boss**: its card, unchanged.

### 5.2 A Pokemon's life, one way

**Box → Partner → Party or Lab → the collection.**

| Stage | Its job | The rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Box** | Waiting, and filling the Pokedex | Most stay here. Catching is for the collection and for opening regions; raising is for the ones you choose |
| **Partner** (1) | Grows | Gets the XP. Evolves at 16 and 36, fully trained at 50. **50 is the cap**: the road to 100 goes |
| **Party** (5) | Fights | Adds to every hit on a leader and the boss (5.3) |
| **Lab desk** (3) | Catches | Fully trained only. Each takes 15% off every wild Pokemon's HP as it appears, 45% with all three (the cap the research system has today). No points, no ranks |
| **Collection** | Your bench | Everyone else. Swap them in when their type is the one you need |

- When a partner is fully trained, **the game asks who is next** instead of taking the oldest in the Box.
- That answers "what after three in the Lab": eight trained Pokemon have a job (five fight, three catch), which is a year or more of training. Past that, a trained Pokemon is a card in your collection that you swap in for a type match-up.
- **Rare Candy**: one level for any Pokemon that is not fully trained, the partner included. One candy at Lv 35 is about two days of work.

### 5.3 Party power: one number

Each of the six adds **1% for every 5 levels** (+10% at Lv 50)*, **doubled when its type beats the foe's**. A Mega doubles that member's share again.

This replaces bond levels, mastery, vitamins, natures, the six signature families, TMs, Type Plates, X items, Dire Hit and critical hits. It is ordinary Pokemon logic (bring Water against Rock), it makes the team screen a real deck choice per leader, and a Lv 1 Pokemon honestly adds nothing, so there is a reason to raise more than one. It also removes the drop when a partner is finished: the trained one keeps adding its 10% from the party.

### 5.4 Each pillar: one track, one prize

**Seven of a pillar earns its prize: its shard or its egg, your choice.***

- **Gym**: the seven pieces of the Strong kit, one per routine. Your original design. A full kit is the prize, every time.
- **Code, Arbor** (Guitar when it is back): seven days done.
- **Sleep**: seven nights of 80 or more, for its egg (it has no shard).

No marks. The five mark currencies, mastery and the five mark shops go. The Dojo, Silph Co., the Garden and the Dream House stay as places: each becomes its pillar's track, seven pips and the prize.

The choice is real: **a shard is power** (towards a Mega), **an egg is a new Pokemon** of that pillar's types.

**Shards come from here and nowhere else**, plus the Comet Shard from the month review as the wildcard.

### 5.5 Mega: the proof you did all your pillars

Key Stone, plus **one shard of each of three colours**, makes a Mega Stone for a fully trained Pokemon at full hearts. It doubles that Pokemon's party power.

One chain: train it (XP), befriend it (hearts), do every pillar (shards).

### 5.6 Hearts: one purpose

Hearts belong to a Pokemon, 0 to 150, drawn as five hearts. They come from days of work with it, and from an Oran Berry. **Full hearts makes it a Best Friend**: it gets its Ribbon and can hold a Mega Stone.

Trainer perks become three, all things you already have: **Pickup** at 10 hearts, **Endure** (one empty day a week costs nothing) at 50*, **+10% XP** at 150*. After that the long game is Best Friends, one Pokemon at a time. That answers "what after the perks": the count of Ribbons in your collection. The bounty goes.

### 5.7 Berries: four jobs instead of ten sizes

All one price*. You choose by what the berry does, and a wild Pokemon takes **one berry**:

| Berry | Job |
|---|---|
| **Oran** | Feed one of yours: +3 hearts |
| **Razz** | Feed the wild Pokemon: 20% of its HP gone now |
| **Nanab** | Feed the wild Pokemon: it stays 7 more days (replaces Max Repel) |
| **Pinap** | Feed the wild Pokemon: if you catch it, it arrives with 10 hearts and 5 gems |

Easier, longer or richer: three answers to one question, and five of one never equals another. This reverses the v14 rule "a berry is food, not bait", on purpose. A berry with one use cannot be a choice.

### 5.8 Balls: a choice of timing

**Great** (50 HP, any day), **Quick** (100 on the day it appears, 25 after), **Timer** (15, and 15 more for each day it has waited), **Master** (earned, never sold). Throw early, throw late, or throw whenever. The other ten go.

### 5.9 Two currencies, two shops

- **Coins**: paid by the day. The **Mart** sells balls, berries and incense.
- **Gems**: paid by milestones. **Rare things** sells the Mystery Egg, Rare Candy, evolution stones, scenes, and at most eight held items that each do something different (Quick Claw, Shell Bell, Lucky Punch, Everstone, Amulet Coin, Rocky Helmet and so on). The +15% and +30% stat items go.

Battle Points and the BP Exchange go.

### 5.10 Streaks: the Frontier only

Momentum stays as the XP multiplier (section 6). For streak rewards, you said the Frontier is fine, so it becomes the one system: a facility per pillar, the Tower for days that count, the Pyramid for Max days. The feats' run-of-7, 30 and 100 go (the Frontier already is that). The weekly quests go (the boss card already is that). The season ladder goes (level milestones already are that).

### 5.11 Legend quests become quests

Each legend is one named deed, printed on its card:

- the seven **Gold Symbols** (already there);
- each **Champion**, on the first lap, not only the rematch;
- every 8th boss and every 25th wild Pokemon, as now.

The 250-BP price list goes.

### 5.12 Stakes

An empty day costs what it costs now. One protection, Endure. Revive, Guard Spec and Focus Sash go.

### 5.13 What this removes

| | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Currencies | 8 | 2 |
| Items | 234 | about 90, most of them Pickup finds |
| Modifiers on a leader hit | 17 | 3 |
| Shops | 8 | 2, and four pillar tracks |
| Streak systems | 4 | 2: momentum and the Frontier |
| Ways to get a shard | 8 | 1, and the Comet |
| Places a trained Pokemon can go | party, desk, "lead again" | party, desk, collection |

Kept exactly as they are: the boss, eggs and the Nursery, Pickup, Sleep, catching and its pace, evolution, the league, the screens' look.

## 6. Momentum: old or new

| | Old (before 5 Nov) | New (v21) |
|---|---|---|
| Rule | Of the last 7 days, how many counted: 3 is ×1.2, 5 is ×1.35, 7 is ×1.5 | A 4-step ladder. A day with two effortful pillars goes up a step, any other day down one, one free rest day a week |
| Can you read it off the screen | Yes: count the lit dots | No: the step and the used-up free day are hidden |
| Speed | Slow up, slow down | Top in 3 days, bottom in 3 |
| Weak spot | 7 of 7 is nearly impossible (you had 0 days at ×1.5 in 39) | Stayed at ×1 through four days of a pillar plus a good night |

**Recommendation: go back to the seven dots, with two fixes.**

1. **The top step at 6 of 7, not 7.** One rest day a week no longer costs the top multiplier. This is what v21's hidden "free day" was for, done where you can see it.
2. **One meaning of "a day that counts", used everywhere**: two pillars, and a good night can be one of them (never alone). That is what your Life OS card already draws.

By that rule you would be at 5 dots and ×1.35 today.

Point 2 reverses a v21 decision ("Sleep never counts towards a tier"). If Sleep is a pillar worth 100 XP on your list, it should count as a pillar. Otherwise the app has two ideas of a pillar, which is bug 4.

## 7. Your questions, answered directly

- **Why did the candy not work?** Bug 1. It cannot be used on anything you own.
- **Why did it not offer the partner?** No version ever let the partner take one. There was no reason for that.
- **Does changing partner lose hearts?** No. It loses the level and bond bonuses instead, and the game does not say so.
- **Where does a trained Pokemon go?** Today: the party, or one of three desk seats with a job in name only, or eight months of "leading again". Proposed: 5.2.
- **What after the perks?** Today: a gap from 350 to about 900 hearts. Proposed: 5.6.
- **What is the difference between the three bars?** How much, how many, what kind. The idea is sound and the game blurs it. Proposed: 5.1.
- **Is the gym counter double-counting?** It is one thing shown twice, on top of a second parallel system (the kit). Proposed: 5.4.

## 8. How to ship it without breaking your history

1. **Bugs first, small and safe.** Candy works on the partner and the Box. One shared "is this allowed, and if not why" check that both the screen and the rules call, so a button that would do nothing is greyed out and says why. The Life OS card, the pay table, the rule book and the stale texts corrected.
2. **Freeze the old rules.** Move today's 2,062-line fold into a file that is never edited again. It computes the state as of the last day of v21. A small conversion turns that state into the new game's state, and a new, short fold runs from there. Old days still replay exactly, and the live code has no version checks in it. This removes cause 2 in section 3.
3. **The conversion keeps what you earned.** Marks become progress on that pillar's track. Removed items are bought back for coins or gems. Hearts, levels, badges and every Pokemon carry over untouched.
4. **One new kind of test:** for every button a screen can show, the action behind it changes the game. This removes cause 3.
5. **Tune in the simulator** before a start date is set, as was done for v21.

Each step is its own piece of work with its own deploy. Step 1 does not depend on any design decision below.

## 9. Decisions that are yours

1. **Momentum**: back to the seven dots with the top at 6 of 7 (recommended), or keep the v21 ladder and fix the Life OS card to draw it.
2. **Sleep**: counts as one of the two pillars for a day that counts (recommended), or stays a bonus.
3. **Scope**: the whole simplification in section 5 (recommended), or bugs only for now.
4. **Seven per track** for a shard or an egg, or a different number.
5. **Berries as bait** (5.7): yes, or keep berries as food only and simply make them one kind.
