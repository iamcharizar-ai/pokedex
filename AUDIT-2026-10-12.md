# Pokedex audit, 12 Oct 2026

Read from the code (core, screens, CSS, service worker, store), not from a run against your real ledger. Each item says what it is, why it matters and roughly how big it is (S under an hour, M a session, L several).

## Done today

- **Stakes are live from 12 Oct** (were 23 Oct). They are the only thing in rules version 8 or 9 that was dated for later. Brought forward to today, not earlier, so no old empty day (3 Oct was one) is punished after the fact. First empty-day cost can land on 14 Oct (two days later, so a late tick still saves it).
- Tap to collect rewards (Claim.tsx), same motion as Life OS.

## 1. Gameplay loops and system design

1. **Claiming is only a hand-over (M).** Rewards are already in the wallet. A real claim would make the day's rewards pending until tapped, which makes opening the app a daily beat. It needs one new ledger event (`claim`) and a fold change in both apps. Highest-value change on the list if you want a reason to open the app each morning.
2. **Nothing to do after the first week (L).** Quests, season and shops all pay in the same few things. The long loops are leaders (about 16 days each by the v8 replay) and graduation. Ideas, in order of value: a weekly "boss" wild Pokemon with a bigger HP bar that needs a streak, a Pokedex completion reward ladder (25, 50, 100 species), and a visible 30-day "trainer rank" that does not reset.
3. **Stakes need a safety valve (S).** With stakes live, a sick day costs HP and XP. A "rest day" you can spend once a week (or a Revive-like item at the Mart, which already exists) should be visible on the Home tab on the morning after an empty day, not only in the Bag. Check that a Revive is easy to find from the slip toast.
4. **Daily pay is the same shape every day (S).** 20 / 45 / 80 coins by share ticked. A small bonus for a second consecutive day above 75% would reward streaks without adding a system.
5. **Month review now pays one Comet Shard (S).** Three months per Mega is slow. Consider 1 shard plus gems, or a Comet Shard at the first review plus one more if the month's graph was above a threshold.
6. **Regions, legendaries and gyms are the real long game (L).** Make the road to the next one the Home screen's headline (distance to next leader, next region, next evolution) so the next goal is always one glance.

## 2. Motion and effects

1. **Every reveal is a fixed full-screen modal (M).** Recap shows one modal for everything new. Small things (a level, a quest) could be non-blocking toasts that slide in from the top bar and fall into the pill that changed, using the same fly helper.
2. **The pills already count up (`Count`), but do not react (S).** Let the pill pulse when a claim lands. The claim chips fly to it, but the number moved earlier, when the day settled.
3. **Tab changes only fade (S).** `tab-in` is a 10px rise. A shared-element feel (the partner sprite staying put while the panels change) would make it feel like one place, not five pages.
4. **No motion on the bar when XP lands (S).** The Life OS bar pulses on arrival; the Pokedex partner bar does not. Add the same `reward-pulse` class and a `data-reward-target="xp"` to the Home XP bar.
5. **Sound is off by default and has six tones (S).** Fine as a default. Add a soft "collect" tone distinct from `catch`, and play a rising pitch on a chain of claims.
6. **Reduced motion is handled in every CSS file (good).** The new fly helper also respects it.
7. **GIF sprites (`/sprites/ani`) animate always (S).** They are the heaviest motion on screen. Pause them off-screen or when the tab is hidden, and offer a still option for low-power phones.

## 3. Visuals and CSS

1. **Four CSS files with overlapping layers (M).** `index.css` (old), `skin.css`, `kit.css`, `economy.css` define the same things at different times (`.partner-bar`-style rules are redefined). One consolidation pass would remove dead rules and make theming possible.
2. **Light theme and large text (M).** The UI is dark-only with small caps labels at 11 to 13px. A check at 200% text size and in a bright room is worthwhile.
3. **Tap targets (S).** The help `?` is 34px. Check everything under 40px; the chips and tab buttons should be fine.
4. **Empty states (S).** With an empty ledger the app shows zeros. A first-run state ("Tick a habit in Life OS to begin") would help a new device or a reset.
5. **Reward and item art (S).** The Comet Shard reuses an existing sprite. A different tint per domain shard on the claim chip would make the strip read faster.

## 4. Accessibility

1. **Focus rings are in place (good).** One global `button, a, summary :focus-visible` rule covers most things. Check the new claim chips and the topbar help button look right with it.
2. **Claim chips and the fly are not announced (S).** Add a polite live region ("Collected 30 coins") when a claim happens.
3. **Colour is carrying meaning (S).** Ready/done states use green/gold only; add a mark or label (the Kit already does this in places).

## 5. Performance and reliability

1. **The whole game re-folds on every event (M).** About 1,000 events is fine; at a year of use it will not be. Fold once per day boundary and cache the result, then apply only today's events on top.
2. **Ledger polling every 60 seconds plus realtime (S).** Fine. Pause the interval when the tab is hidden.
3. **Service worker caches sprites forever (fine).** Bump `SPRITES` when a sprite is replaced; otherwise a changed sprite will never show on an installed app.
4. **Local-only state is scattered (S).** `pokedex.seen.v1`, `pokedex.claimed.v1`, `pokedex.sound.v1` are all per device. Fine, but list them in the Guide so a reset is understood.
5. **Tests (S).** 57 tests cover the core. Nothing covers the screens; one smoke test that loads the app with a fixture ledger and checks the Claim strip would have caught nothing today but would catch regressions as the UI grows.

## 6. Housekeeping

- `tsconfig.tsbuildinfo` is already in `.gitignore` but is still tracked, so it shows as modified after every build. `git rm --cached tsconfig.tsbuildinfo` ends that.
- `archive/World.tsx` and `public/world` are unused; keep the archive, but drop the unused public assets from the deployed bundle if they are large.
- The Guide (Life OS `docs/GUIDE.md`) says stakes are live from 12 Oct now; the older sections 5 and 15 are history by design.
