# Rules version 13: Champion's Road

In force from **15 Oct 2026** (`V13_START` in `core/rules.ts`). Every day before it folds exactly as it did.

The aim of v13 is that **every system means something**. Before it:

- Hearts stopped mattering after three bond levels.
- A forged Mega Stone was a flat +10%.
- Gigantamax was only a picture on a perfect day.
- The partner's level counted for nothing in a fight.
- Coins piled up with nothing to buy.
- There was nothing to do once the ninth Champion fell.

v13 fixes each of these and adds a long game: the Battle Frontier, Legend quests and Rematch ranks.

## 1. What a strike is now

A strike is the day's work against the gym leader. It is still the day's habits, each counted extra if the leader is weak to that domain and extra if the partner's type beats the leader's. On top of that, every bonus below is **added** together and then applied once.

| Source | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Partner level | +1% per level (Lv 50 is +50%) |
| Each party member | +2%, plus 0.2% per level of that member, plus 1% per bond level of that member |
| Partner bond | +5% per bond level (5 levels) |
| Forged Mega Stone | +10%, plus 5% per bond level |
| Mega for the day (full momentum and every pillar done, no stone) | +10% |
| Vitamins given to the partner | +3% each on that domain (10 at most per domain) |
| Held item, mastery, natures | as before |
| X items (today only) | +20%, +40%, +60% or +120% on one domain |

Then two **day effects** multiply the strike:

- **Critical hit:** from bond level 3, about one day in eight. The chance rises with Dire Hit and the Scope Lens. A critical day's strike is ×1.5.
- **Dynamax:** a perfect day Dynamaxes the partner, and its strike is ×2. A partner with a **Gigantamax** form strikes ×2.5.

**Leaders get tougher as you go:**

- HP is 2,000 × (1 + 0.15 per league in) × 1.25 for the Elite Four or 1.6 for the Champion.
- So a Kanto gym leader has 2,000 HP and the Paldea Champion about 7,500.
- Growth from levels, the party, the bond and vitamins keeps pace with this.

## 2. Hearts (bond): five levels, each with a perk

| Hearts | Bond | Perk |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | **Pickup**: on any day with a pillar, the partner may find an item (30% at bond 1, +10% per level) |
| 30 | 2 | **Endure**: one empty day a week costs nothing |
| 60 | 3 | **Focus**: critical hits start |
| 100 | 4 | **Best friends**: +10% XP |
| 150 | 5 | **Exp. Share**: everyone in the party who is still being raised gets 10% of the partner's daily XP |

Ways to earn hearts:

- **Every pillar** done while it is your partner: +1. A four-pillar day is four hearts.
- Oran Berry: +1. Sitrus Berry: +3.
- The six friendship berries (Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot, Hondew, Grepa, Tamato): +5 each.
- Sweet Heart: +3. Heart Scale: +2.

## 3. Items that matter now

- **Pickup finds** get better the further you are in the leagues:
  - Treasures, which the **Collector** buys for coins.
  - Fossils, which the **Lab** revives into Pokemon.
  - Evolution stones and evolution items.
  - Friendship berries, Honey, Heart Scales and Rare Candy.
- **Evolution items:** any stone or trade item evolves a Pokemon in My Pokemon by one stage, if the next form shares one of the item's types. Leaf Stone works on Grass, Dusk Stone on Dark or Ghost, Dragon Scale on Dragon, and so on. Your partner still evolves only by your work.
- **Rare Candy:** +1 level for a Pokemon in My Pokemon.
- **Vitamins**, bought in the domain shops with marks: Calcium for code, Protein for gym, Zinc for guitar, Carbos for Arbor, HP Up for sleep. Each one makes that domain's work hit 3% harder, for the Pokemon you give it to, for good.
- **X items**, battle items from the Mart: one day's boost to one domain against the leader.
  - X Sp. Atk for code, X Attack for gym, X Sp. Def for guitar, X Defend for Arbor, X Speed for chores, X Accuracy for sleep.
  - Three strengths are sold in the Mart; a fourth (×6) only from the Frontier.
- **Dire Hit:** raises today's critical-hit chance. **Guard Spec:** forgives the next empty day.
- **Honey:** the next wild Pokemon is a rare one.
- **Everstone:** a partner holding it keeps its form and keeps levelling.

## 4. Mega Evolution, the fair way

- A Mega Stone takes **three shards of three different colours** (red, blue, yellow, green). A Comet Shard fills any socket.
- For a Pokemon with two Megas (Charizard, Mewtwo), you choose X or Y. Each needs its own stone.
- A forged stone is now worth +10% plus 5% per bond level, so a partner you are close to hits much harder.

## 5. The Battle Frontier (Journey tab)

Seven facilities, each a **streak** of one kind of day, and each with a Frontier Brain:

| Facility | Brain | A win is | Rest days forgiven | Silver / Gold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Battle Tower | Palmer | a day with two pillars | 1 | 14 / 35 |
| Battle Factory | Noland | a code day | 2 | 10 / 30 |
| Battle Arena | Greta | a gym day | 2 | 10 / 30 |
| Battle Palace | Spenser | a guitar day | 2 | 10 / 30 |
| Battle Dome | Tucker | an Arbor morning | 2 | 10 / 30 |
| Battle Pike | Lucy | a good night (80+) | 1 | 7 / 21 |
| Battle Pyramid | Brandon | a perfect day (counted, not a streak) | n/a | 5 / 15 |

- **Battle Points (BP):** every win pays 1 BP, and every seventh win in a row a bonus: 7 BP at seven, 14 at fourteen, up to 35. The Pyramid pays 3 BP per perfect day.
- **Symbols:** a Silver Symbol pays 25 BP and gems. A Gold Symbol pays 60 BP and a **Legend key**.
- The **BP Exchange** sells:
  - Competitive held items: Expert Belt, Focus Sash, Rocky Helmet, Scope Lens, Quick Claw, Shell Bell, Wide Lens and Zoom Lens.
  - Exp. Share, the ×6 X items, trade evolution items and fossils.
  - Legend keys (250 BP) and a Master Ball (600 BP).

## 6. Legend quests

- A **Legend key** (an orb, a ticket, a flute, a bell) summons a legendary as the next wild Pokemon. You catch it by your work, like any other wild Pokemon.
- There are 26 keys. Some examples:
  - Red Orb: Groudon. Blue Orb: Kyogre. Jade Orb: Rayquaza.
  - Adamant Orb: Dialga. Lustrous Orb: Palkia. Griseous Orb: Giratina.
  - Rainbow Wing: Ho-Oh. Tidal Bell: Lugia. Azure Flute: Arceus.
  - Old Sea Map: Mew. Member Card: Darkrai. Meteorite: Deoxys. Gracidea: Shaymin. Lock Capsule: Jirachi.
  - and more.
- Where keys come from: Gold Symbols, Rematch Champions, and the BP Exchange.

## 7. After the ninth Champion: Rematch ranks

- When Paldea's Champion falls, every league comes back at **rank ★2**, then ★3 and so on.
- Each rank adds 60% to every leader's HP.
- Rematch wins pay gems and BP, and every Rematch Champion pays a Legend key.
- Each rank's Champion goes into the Hall of Fame.
- The game never runs out.

## 8. New feats

Eleven feats for the new systems:

- Bond 5, 25 Pickup finds, a fossil revived, an item evolution, ten vitamins.
- A first Silver Symbol, a first Gold Symbol, three legendaries caught.
- Five Dynamax days, ten critical hits, a ★2 Champion.
