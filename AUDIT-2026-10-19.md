# Audit, 19 Oct 2026: choices that aren't choices, and the morning sync

Two questions:

1. Which systems look like a decision but have only one sensible move, the way berries do?
2. Why does Life OS take so long to start the day in the morning, and will the band fix it?

Nothing has been changed yet. This file holds the findings and what I propose.

---

## Part 1: choices that aren't choices

### The test

Keeping an item in the bag only means something if keeping it could be worth more than using it now. That takes at least one of these:

- **Another use.** You could spend it on something else.
- **Timing.** It is worth more on some days than on others.
- **Target.** It is worth more on one Pokemon than on another.
- **Scarcity.** There are limited slots or uses, so you have to pick.

If an item has none of these, the only sensible move is to use it right away. The bag just adds a click.

### 1. Berries: fails all four (you were right)

- `core/fold.ts:764` (`case 'feed'`): a berry does one thing, adds hearts to the partner. Hearts never go down, and they never matter less later.
- The partner is the only target.
- No day is better than another for feeding.
- The bag popup even says **"Feed it now"** (`src/Play.tsx:501`).
- Two smaller faults make it worse:
  - **The six "domain" friendship berries have no domain effect.** Hondew is "the code berry" in name only. It works exactly like Kelpsy.
  - **Hearts past 150 are thrown away without a word.** Bond 5 is the cap, and feeding at 150+ still uses up the berry.

**The one real decision is hidden:** a partner's hearts stay with it after it graduates. A party member adds 1% to every strike per bond level (`BOND_PARTY`), but the only way to earn hearts is to be the partner. Party bond is a real stat that nothing lets you invest in.

**Proposed fix (recommended): berries are both food and bait.**

- **Feed any Pokemon you own, not only the partner.**
  - Feeding the partner raises its perks: Pickup, Endure, Focus, and so on.
  - Feeding a party member raises the party bonus.
  - That gives you a choice of who gets the berry.
  - Feeding a Pokemon already at 150 hearts is refused, with the reason shown.
- **Use a berry on the weekly boss** before a ball, the way Razz Berries work.
  - It raises that throw's catch chance. Oran +5%, Sitrus +10%, a friendship berry +10%.
  - A friendship berry gives **+20% when its domain matches the boss's types**, so Hondew helps against a Psychic/Electric/Ghost boss. This gives the domain names a job.
  - At most one berry per throw. There are still only three throws a week.
- **Result:** every berry asks a question. Hearts now, for good? Or keep it for the boss week with a Pokemon you want?
  - Both options have value.
  - Both get stronger later: bond perks add up over time, and boss weeks bring rarer Pokemon.
  - So holding a berry and using it now are both reasonable plays.

**Simpler option:** feed berries automatically when they come in, the same way XP works. That is honest, and it removes a fake choice without adding a real one.

### 2. Treasures: a coin cheque with a delay

`case 'sell'`: a treasure only ever turns into coins, at a fixed price. Coins never lose value, so you should always sell straight away. "Sell all" is just a chore.

**Fix:** the **Collector's wanted list.**

- Each day one or two kinds of treasure sell for **×2**.
- The list is seeded by date, so every device shows the same one.
- This makes timing matter: a Big Nugget is worth holding until the day it's wanted.

### 3. Vitamins on a graduate are wasted for good: a trap, not a choice

- Strikes count only **`partner.vits`** (`core/fold.ts:1012`).
- A Pokemon that has graduated can never lead again: `lead` only takes Pokemon from the queue.
- But `MonExtras` (`src/Champion.tsx:251`) offers vitamins to *any* Pokemon, graduates included.
- A vitamin given to a graduate does nothing, ever.

**Fix (pick one):**

- **(a)** Block it in the fold and in the UI.
- **(b, better)** Make a party member's vitamins count at **⅓ strength** on its strikes. Then a vitamin on a graduate becomes a real choice: a smaller bonus, but one that stays in the party for good.

### 4. Checked and fine (real decisions already)

- **Mastery vs vitamins.**
  - The first two mastery levels cost 4 and 8 marks and help every Pokemon, so they beat a 10-mark vitamin.
  - From level 3 on, vitamins are cheaper per % but help only one Pokemon.
  - That's a real trade-off.
- **X items, Dire Hit:** you pick the day. **Guard Spec, Revive, Max Repel:** you hold them as insurance.
- **Honey, incense and Legend keys:** each one sets the next wild Pokemon, and only one can be active at a time, so you choose which to use.
- **Rare Candy and evolution items:** you choose who gets them.
- **X or Y Mega:** a real choice.
- **Collect strip:** a ceremony rather than a decision, which is fine.

### 5. A sleep rule that broke on the way

The sleep design (`SLEEP-DESIGN.md` D2) said **"no reading is not a miss"**: if the band has no reading, you can tick the sleep habit yourself for 30 XP.

Version 13 removed that. Now the Sleep row never accepts a hand tick (Life OS `App.tsx`, `toggle`). So if the band is dead, or the sign-in has expired, the pillar is lost. That costs:

- a counting day (two pillars),
- the Battle Pike streak,
- Endure.

**Fix:** bring back the fallback. If there's no reading by the end of the day, allow a self-reported tick at core XP (30), with no Bells.

## Part 2: the morning sync

### What actually happens (from the ledger, last 6 mornings)

| Morning | Woke | Sleep row landed | Wait |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14 Oct | 08:20 | 09:06 | 46 min |
| 15 Oct | 07:30 | 07:58 (you pressed Start my day 20 s before) | 28 min |
| 16 Oct | 08:20 | 08:41 | 21 min |
| 17 Oct | 08:50 | **23:40** | **15 h** (the day only rolled at 14:00) |
| 18 Oct | 07:50 | 08:33 | 43 min |
| 19 Oct | 08:20 | 08:50 | 30 min |

The chain the reading goes through:

```
phone / band ──► Google Health app ──► Google cloud ──► webhook ──► Vercel fn ──► ledger ──► Life OS
   (1) upload: only when the app is opened      (2) Google's    (3) seconds    (4) Life OS reads it
                                                   delay
```

1. **The upload is the step you control by hand.** Google gets last night only after the Google Health app syncs. Right now that happens when you open it. On 17 Oct it didn't happen until late at night.
2. **Google's processing and webhook delay.** I can't measure this from here. It's probably minutes.
3. **The Vercel function** reads Google and writes the row in seconds. It's fine.
4. **Life OS reading the ledger.** This is the slow open you notice, and it's entirely our fault:
   - A cold open of Life OS (`cloudSync.ts` `boot`) downloads the **entire ledger, every time**: 28,941 rows, about **10.3 MB**. That took 5.5 s on this PC's connection, and will take longer on a phone on mobile data, plus the time to parse it.
   - The day can't change until that download finishes.
   - **62% of it (6.4 MB) is 27,096 `guitar_plan` rows that one Woodshed device wrote on 9–10 Oct**, a runaway loop. It has written about one a day since, so that loop is fixed, but the rows are still there.
   - Another 3.4 MB is 327 full copies of the habit config. Only the newest one is ever used.
   - The Pokedex downloads `guitar_plan` too.
   - When Life OS is already open in the background, realtime brings the row in within seconds. Only the cold open is slow.

**The deeper problem is the design:** the day *waits* on a reading that depends on Google, and nothing on our side can make Google faster. Every delay in steps 1–2 shows up as a frozen day.

### Will the band fix it?

**Only partly, and I can't check it until you have it.**

- **What should get better:**
  - The Fitbit Air syncs to the Google Health app over Bluetooth in the background, so step 1 should happen without you opening the app.
  - For that to work, the app needs background activity allowed and battery optimisation switched off for it.
  - The readings will also be real sleep stages, not estimates from the phone. (Your wake times now are all round 10-minute marks.)
- **What won't change:**
  - Background syncs are spaced out, and the night is scored only after a sync that comes after you wake. So "within a few minutes of waking" isn't guaranteed. Some mornings will still mean 20–40 minutes, or opening the app.
  - It does nothing for step 4, the 10 MB cold open.

### Proposed redesign, in order of payoff

**R1. Stop the day from waiting on the reading (game rule).**

- After midnight, it stays yesterday until **your usual wake time minus 2 hours**:
  - "usual wake time" = the median wake time of your last 7 measured nights,
  - limited to between 04:00 and 07:00,
  - with a fixed 05:00 before there's any history.
- A reading or **Start my day** still starts the day earlier.
- The 14:00 fallback goes away.
- With your nights (waking 07:30–08:50), the day starts on its own at about 06:00. When you wake, it's already today.
- The Sleep pillar shows "waiting for the band" and fills in by itself when the row arrives.
- Everything that reads sleep is worked out again from the ledger: the counting day, the Pike, the boss's sleep task, momentum. So a late reading changes nothing except when you see it.
- This keeps the idea behind "the day ends when you wake", and drops the waiting.

**R2. Make Life OS open fast (engineering, no rule change).**

- Start from the local cache plus a saved cursor, and fetch only rows newer than the cursor.
- Fetch the rows that decide the day first (`vitals` and `day_start` for the last two days), so the date changes in under a second.
- On a cold open, fetch only the newest `config` row, as the Pokedex already does.
- **Clean up the 27k runaway `guitar_plan` rows.** `guitar_plan` is a full snapshot, so only the newest one per day matters.
  - This deletes rows from the shared ledger.
  - So: back it up first, and **only with your go-ahead.**

**R3. A "check now" endpoint.**

- Add `/api/health-sync`, the same code as the webhook's sync.
- While Today shows "waiting for the band", Life OS calls it when you open the app, and then every 2 minutes while it's on screen. It's rate-limited on the server.
- This means:
  - The webhook stops being a single point of failure.
  - You stop opening Vitals to check whether the reading landed.

**R4. Say what's being waited on.** The check-now call can return whether Google has last night yet. Today then shows one of two lines:

- "Google hasn't got last night yet: open Google Health to push it."
- "Got it, scoring…"

That replaces the open-Google-Health, refresh, open-Vitals routine.

**R5. Bring back the self-reported fallback** (Part 1 §5).
